# Amazon Robotics: regional source evidence

Verified 2026-09-19. One canonical `amazon-robotics` record appears in the
Boston and Bay Area views, with shared careers information and separate city pins.

## Identity and shared links

Amazon's public robotics news hub is titled **Amazon Robotics** and is the
record's website destination.[3]
The permanent Amazon Jobs team page is **Amazon Robotics** and describes
robotics, mechatronics and software for fulfillment automation. It states:
“Located in the greater Boston area, we’re the epicenter of robotics innovation.”
It also describes co-located offices, labs, test floors and factories.[1]

Use that robotics-specific team page for the shared careers link, not an
individual vacancy or Amazon's unfiltered global jobs homepage. The old
`/en/teams/amazon-robotics` route redirects to the verified `/content/en/teams/ftr/amazon-robotics`
route.[1] The former standalone corporate domain did not provide a usable public
company page during this check and is not used.

## Boston: North Reading, Massachusetts

The current official **Senior Electrical Engineer, Amazon Robotics** posting,
job 10526837, identifies the Amazon Robotics team and lists “USA, MA, North
Reading”. It describes design and manufacturing work on industrial robotic
systems and components.[2]
The corresponding official jobs-search record has `type: ONSITE`,
`normalizedCityName: North Reading`, and `normalizedStateName: Massachusetts`.[6]
Together with the robotics team's regional description, this supports the
North Reading city pin. It is not a claim that every Boston-area Amazon
Robotics job or facility is in North Reading.

## Bay Area: San Francisco, California

The current official **Lab Safety Engineer, Amazon Robotics, FF Tech Enable and
Solutions** posting, job 10528946, lists San Francisco and the Amazon Robotics
team. Its responsibilities include “Owning EHS compliance for a robotics research
and development lab” and reporting to the “AR EHS Global Lab Safety Manager”.[4]
Its official search metadata explicitly marks the location `ONSITE` and gives
`normalizedCityName: San Francisco` and `normalizedStateName: California`.[5]

This is direct robotics-team lab evidence, not an inference from generic Amazon
corporate offices, AWS, unrelated Lab126 hardware roles, or Zoox. No other Bay
municipality is implied. The two differently capitalized location entries in the
job data describe the same city, not two offices or two company records.

## Mapping and maintenance

Both pins are **approximate municipality points**, not office entrances.
North Reading uses an OpenStreetMap/Nominatim result matched by town name,
Massachusetts state and municipal address type. San Francisco reuses the same
cached city point as other San Francisco records, preserving shared-marker
behavior rather than introducing artificial offsets.

Individual job URLs are location evidence only. Vacancies can close; the durable
robotics team page remains the careers destination. Recheck locations when
updating verification dates. No private outreach history or personal contact
information is included.
