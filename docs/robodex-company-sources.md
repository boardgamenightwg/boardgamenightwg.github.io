# Additional Robodex company sources

Public sources checked **2026-09-19**.

This batch adds robotics hardware, autonomy, and robotics-development companies beyond the existing host-derived roster. Inclusion is not a claim of club hosting, endorsement, current vacancies, or a personal relationship. Company information comes only from public sources; private correspondence and contact details are not published.

Permanent careers pages may list no vacancies. Unknown careers destinations remain explicit nulls; unverified cities remain unmapped. City points are approximate municipalities, not office entrances or an assertion that all jobs are based there.
