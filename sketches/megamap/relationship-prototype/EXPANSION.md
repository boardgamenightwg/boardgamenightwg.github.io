# Candidate communities beyond the current prototype

## BU RASTIC — included as a candidate
BU calls RASTIC a collaborative workspace and community. Its published offerings include student seminars/workshops, projects and networking with peers, faculty and industry. It is not included merely because it hosted BGNWG. External drop-in eligibility has not been established; the card says student-centered.
- https://www.bu.edu/rastic/
- https://www.bu.edu/eng/academics/teaching-and-innovation/rastic/what-we-offer/

## Artisans Asylum — strongest next addition
A nonprofit maker community in its own right, with classes, shared shops (including electronics/robotics), memberships and day-pass entry points. This is more than a venue listing. Training and tool-access rules still matter.
- https://www.artisansasylum.com/

## Cambridge Hackspace — strong next addition
A project-making community whose published membership includes tools, project storage, around-the-clock member access and peer support. Its join page separates paid membership benefits from general participation; do not imply all access is free.
- https://www.cambridgehackspace.com/join

## The Foundry — useful broader-boundary candidate
A Cambridge creativity/collaboration community spanning science, technology, engineering, arts and math. It offers maker spaces and programs with access initiatives and income-based sliding-scale memberships. Broader than robotics, making it a useful scope question rather than an automatic inclusion.
- https://www.cambridgefoundry.org/about

Only RASTIC is shown as a candidate in this iteration. The other three are researched recommendations, not added nodes or presumed partners. None of these facts creates a relationship to another group by itself.
